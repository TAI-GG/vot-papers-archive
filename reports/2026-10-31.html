Don't Risk It!: Trajectory-Conditioned Distributional Risk for LiDAR-Only Crowd Navigation

不要冒险！：仅使用 LiDAR 的人群导航的轨迹条件分布风险
arXiv: 2610.40050 · 2026-10-29 · Mathai Mathew Pulicken, Laksh Nanwani, Basant Sharma, Phani Teja Singamaneni, Arun Kumar Singh, K. Madhava Krishna
[EN] Efficient crowd navigation requires anticipating dynamic collision-free space without relying on fragile object tracking. We propose the Don't Risk It Planner (DRIP), an end-to-end LiDAR-only planning framework that couples occupancy forecasting with trajectory-conditioned clearance and uncertainty estimation. Operating directly on LiDAR-derived occupancy grid histories, DRIP predicts a distribution over minimum robot-obstacle clearance for candidate control sequences to evaluate collision risk. The model is trained using a risk cost that grounds predictive uncertainty directly in empirical collision outcomes rather than geometric regression alone. In closed-loop experiments with up to 45 pedestrians, DRIP achieves higher success rates with fewer collisions and reduced freezing behavior compared to state-of-the-art baselines.
[中文] 高效的人群导航需要预测动态的无碰撞空间，而不依赖于脆弱的物体跟踪。我们提出了 Don’t Risk It Planner (DRIP)，这是一种仅使用激光雷达的端到端规划框架，它将占用率预测与轨迹条件间隙和不确定性估计结合起来。DRIP 直接对 LiDAR 导出的占用网格历史进行操作，预测候选控制序列的最小机器人障碍物间隙分布，以评估碰撞风险。该模型使用风险成本进行训练，该风险成本将预测不确定性直接基于经验碰撞结果，而不是仅基于几何回归。在最多 45 名行人的闭环实验中，与最先进的基准相比，DRIP 实现了更高的成功率、更少的碰撞和减少的冻结行为。

HARP: Handoff-Aware Recovery Planning for Active Target Acquisition in Multi-UAV Handoff

HARP：多无人机切换中主动目标捕获的切换感知恢复规划
arXiv: 2610.33640 · 2026-10-24 · Heegyeong Kim, Alice James, Avishkar Seth, Endrowednes Kuantama, Richard Han
[EN] Continuous aerial tracking often requires multiple drones to maintain target coverage over long periods. During handoff, the receiving UAV may lack prior target observations and must actively acquire a usable view without disrupting the sender's ongoing coverage. This work introduces HARP, a handoff-aware recovery planner that registers bounded sender predictions in the receiver's local map, distinguishes field-of-view failure from physical occlusion, and generates failure-specific yaw, translation, and parallax viewpoints. Candidate motions are checked for receiver clearance, sender-fiducial observability, protected sender--target visibility, temporal validity, and deadline compliance. When a complete trajectory cannot be admitted, HARP may execute a sampled admissible nonzero prefix and replan from updated evidence. In simulation, HARP acquired the target in all 49 standard trials and in 15 of 16 stress trials. Field trials validated HARP's recovery geometry under field-of-view limitation and environmental occlusions.
[中文] 连续空中跟踪通常需要多架无人机来长时间维持目标覆盖范围。在切换期间，接收无人机可能缺乏先前的目标观测，并且必须主动获取可用视图，而不会中断发送者的持续覆盖范围。这项工作引入了 HARP，它是一种切换感知恢复规划器，可在接收器的本地地图中注册有界发送器预测，区分视场故障与物理遮挡，并生成特定于故障的偏航、平移和视差视点。检查候选动议的接收者许可、发送者基准可观察性、受保护的发送者目标可见性、时间有效性和截止日期合规性。当无法承认完整的轨迹时，HARP 可以执行采样的可接受的非零前缀并根据更新的证据重新规划。在模拟中，HARP 在所有 49 次标准试验和 16 次压力试验中的 15 次中实现了目标。现场试验验证了 HARP 在视场限制和环境遮挡下的恢复几何形状。
