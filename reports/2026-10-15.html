[EN] Multi-robot coordination is essential in applications such as coverage, target tracking, and information gathering, where robots must jointly select actions to maximize team performance. Submodular maximization provides an effective framework for such coordination, and resilient variants further account for robot failures or adversarial removals. However, existing methods typically overlook the impact of robot loss on the communication network, which may disconnect the surviving team and prevent continued coordination. To address this issue, we introduce Resilient $k$-Connected Submodular Maximization, which jointly considers task performance, resilience, and communication connectivity.
