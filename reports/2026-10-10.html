Continuous Ground-Truth Construction and a Recovery Policy for Air--Water Robotic Tracking

连续地面真实施工和空气回收政策--水机器人跟踪
arXiv: 2610.11096 · 2026-10-08 · Jiangong Xiao, Zhe Sun, Kanzhong Yao, Yuanbo Bi, Haofei Zhao, Ruixuan Hu
[EN] Visual tracking across the air-water interface is challenged by splashes, bubbles, refraction, reflections, and abrupt appearance changes that can temporarily invalidate observations. This setting poses two coupled difficulties: first, for evaluation, image-only annotation cannot reliably describe the target's physical location during visual blindness; second, for online tracking, corrupted observations can contaminate motion estimates and appearance templates. We address the first difficulty with a construction pipeline that synchronizes camera frames with motion-capture poses, projects known target geometry, corrects underwater projection with a medium-gated residual, and subjects the annotations to manual review. This yields an evaluation-only cross-medium test set of 22,346 frames. We further introduce a Cross-Medium Recovery Policy (CMRP) centered on confidence-triggered template selection. It supplies MixFormerV2 with the fixed initial template, a window-best pre-trigger template, and a trigger-frame Kalman-guided image crop, together with their associated weights, without retraining the visual backbone. In the accuracy evaluation, CMRP achieves 49.90 Macro Success AUC, 2.95 points above MixFormerV2 Official. On selected cross-medium transition and occlusion-recovery intervals, CMRP increases MixFormerV2 tracking coverage from 47.91\% to 50.43\% relative to Official updat
[中文] 通过空气-水界面的视觉跟踪受到飞溅、气泡、折射、反射和突然外观变化的挑战，这些变化可能会暂时使观测无效。这种设置带来了两个耦合的困难：首先，对于评估，仅图像注释无法可靠地描述视盲期间目标的物理位置；其次，对于在线跟踪，损坏的观测可能会污染运动估计和外观模板。我们通过建设管道解决了第一个难题，该管道使相机帧与运动捕捉姿势同步，投影已知的目标几何形状，使用中等门控残差校正水下投影，并对注释进行手动审核。这产生了22,346帧的仅评估跨媒体测试集。我们进一步引入了以置信触发模板选择为中心的跨介质恢复策略（ CMRP ）。它为MixFormerV2提供固定的初始模板、窗口最佳的预触发模板和触发帧卡尔曼引导的图像裁剪，以及它们的相关权重，而无需重新训练视觉主干。在准确性评估中， CMRP达到49.90宏观成功AUC ，比MixFormerV2 Official高2.95分。在选定的跨媒体转换和遮挡恢复间隔上， CMRP将MixFormerV2跟踪覆盖率相对于官方更新从47.91\ %提高到50.43\ %

MOTIP2: Spatial Priors for End-to-End Multi-Object Tracking

MOTIP2 ：用于端到端多对象跟踪的空间先验
arXiv: 2610.10391 · 2026-10-07 · Benoît Roussel, Damien Bouet, Liming Chen, Pierre Perrault
[EN] End-to-end multi-object trackers have narrowed the gap with classical tracking-by-detection on association-difficult benchmarks. Yet they still make spatially implausible errors no classical tracker would, such as assigning one identity to objects on opposite sides of the frame. A model could learn to avoid them, but tracking annotations are scarce, so we encode spatial priors explicitly instead, while keeping inference fully end-to-end with no post-hoc association. We propose three spatial priors, at the data, loss, and representation stages. Spatial ID Switches bias trajectory permutations toward spatially overlapping objects, reducing the mismatch between training and inference confusions. Spatial ID Loss scales each identity's penalty by its box distance, so a distant switch costs more than a nearby one. Spatial Anchor gives each track token its frame position, an explicit spatial cue for attention. We instantiate the three priors in MOTIP2, a tracker adapted from MOTIP and built on the real-time DEIM detection transformer. Trained without extra data, its main model, MOTIP2-L, sets a new state of the art: 73.4 HOTA on DanceTrack, 76.0 on SportsMOT, and 71.1 IDF1 on PersonPath22. MOTIP2 is a family of models spanning the speed-accuracy trade-off: a lighter model, MOTIP2-S, matches the original MOTIP at over 3x the speed, and MOTIP2-X reaches 74.8 HOTA on DanceTrack.
[中文] 端到端多对象跟踪器已经缩小了与关联困难基准的经典逐检测跟踪的差距。然而，它们仍然会产生经典跟踪器无法实现的空间错误，例如将一个身份分配给框架相对两侧的物体。模型可以学会避免它们，但跟踪注释很少，因此我们显式编码空间先验，同时保持推理完全端到端，没有事后关联。我们在数据、损失和表示阶段提出了三个空间先验。空间ID切换将轨迹排列偏向空间重叠的对象，减少训练和推理混淆之间的不匹配。空间ID丢失会根据每个身份的盒子距离来调整其惩罚，因此远距离切换的成本高于附近的切换。Spatial Anchor为每个轨道标记提供其帧位置，这是一个明确的空间注意力提示。我们在MOTIP2中实例化了三个先验，这是一个从MOTIP改编而来的跟踪器，建立在实时DEIM检测变压器之上。在没有额外数据的情况下进行培训，其主模型MOTIP2-L奠定了新的先进水平： DanceTrack上的73.4 HOTA ， SportsMOT上的76.0和PersonPath22上的71.1 IDF1。MOTIP2是一个跨越速度精度权衡的型号系列：更轻的型号MOTIP2-S以超过3倍的速度匹配原始MOTIP ， MOTIP2-X在DanceTrack上达到74.8 HOTA。

Recon2Servo: Robotic Ultrasound Visual Servoing via Learned Image-to-Motion Inference

Recon2Servo ：通过学习图像到运动推理的机器人超声视觉伺服
arXiv: 2610.06010 · 2026-10-05 · Yameng Zhang, Pei Liu, Dianye Huang, Yizhao Qian, Zhongyu Chen, Xiangyu Chu
[EN] Ultrasound visual servoing is essential for autonomous robotic ultrasound, yet 6-DoF probe control from 2D B-mode images remains challenging due to limited and ambiguous out-of-plane motion cues. Existing methods typically rely on anatomical priors or handcrafted visual features, limiting their generalizability across imaging targets. Inspired by trackerless 3D ultrasound reconstruction, we propose Recon2Servo, a visual servoing framework that learns image-to-motion inference directly from B-mode images for 6-DoF probe control. A DINOv3 encoder with low-rank adaptation and a bidirectional relation module estimate the relative probe pose between current and target images to guide iterative closed-loop target-view alignment. The framework combines supervised relative-pose learning, reconstruction-guided closed-loop adaptation, and bounded residual pose correction to improve motion inference during servoing. Evaluations on a public dataset and an in-house dataset collected from 12 healthy volunteers using different ultrasound systems demonstrate its effectiveness in reconstructed-volume servoing. Additional real-robot demonstrations of target-view alignment and dynamic tracking on a human forearm are provided in the supplementary video: https://youtu.be/qwsOdI-GMYk.
[中文] 超声视觉伺服对于自主机器人超声是必不可少的，但由于有限且模糊的平面外运动线索，来自2D B模式图像的6自由度探头控制仍然具有挑战性。现有方法通常依赖于解剖学先验或手工制作的视觉特征，限制了它们在成像目标上的可推广性。受无追踪器3D超声重建的启发，我们提出了Recon2Servo ，这是一种视觉伺服框架，可直接从B模式图像中学习图像到运动的推理，用于6自由度探头控制。具有低秩自适应和双向关系模块的DINOv3编码器估计当前和目标图像之间的相对探头姿势，以指导迭代闭环目标-视图对准。该框架结合了监督相对位置学习、重构引导闭环自适应和有限残差位姿校正，以改善伺服过程中的运动推理。对使用不同超声系统从12名健康志愿者收集的公共数据集和内部数据集的评估证明了其在重建体积伺服中的有效性。补充视频中提供了人体前臂上目标视图对准和动态跟踪的其他实时机器人演示： https://youtu.be/qwsOdI-GMYk。

Referring Multi-Object Tracking in Moving-Camera Videos via Global Motion Compensation

通过全局运动补偿引用移动摄像机视频中的多对象跟踪
arXiv: 2610.04185 · 2026-10-03 · Hsin-Chen Pai, Jyun-Kai Wang, Yi-Cheng Peng, Wei-Ta Chu
[EN] Referring multi-object tracking (RMOT) takes a video and a language expression as input and tracks all referred objects. Many tracking requirements involve how an object moves rather than how it appears. However, in a video captured by a moving camera, a parked vehicle may appear to move, while a moving vehicle may show little displacement. Existing RMOT methods relate motion with text but do not explicitly remove camera-induced motion. In this paper, we propose extracting residual motion across frames by estimating camera motion in driver-view videos and compare motion characteristics with the query expression. We consider the motion-matching extent and integrate it with the RMOT method's prediction result through late fusion. In the evaluation, we verify the performance gain of taking the motion compensation module as a plug-in across different RMOT hosts.
[中文] 引用多对象跟踪（ RMOT ）以视频和语言表达式作为输入，并跟踪所有引用的对象。许多跟踪要求涉及物体的移动方式，而不是物体的外观。然而，在移动摄像机拍摄的视频中，停放的车辆可能看起来在移动，而移动的车辆可能显示出很小的位移。现有的RMOT方法将运动与文本相关联，但没有明确移除摄像头引起的运动。在本文中，我们提出通过估计驾驶员视图视频中的摄像机运动来提取跨帧的残留运动，并将运动特性与查询表达式进行比较。我们考虑运动匹配程度，并通过后期融合将其与RMOT方法的预测结果集成。在评估中，我们验证了将运动补偿模块作为不同RMOT主机的插件的性能提升。

RACER: Residual-Adaptive Closed-Loop Estimation for Sampling-Based Planning in Wheeled-Quadruped Racing

RACER ：轮式四足赛车中基于抽样规划的残差自适应闭环估计
arXiv: 2610.07409 · 2026-10-05 · Yuxiang Liu, Marla Eisman, Lizhi Yang, Aaron Ames, Francesco Borrelli
[EN] We present RACER, a hierarchical control framework for wheel-based quadruped racing that combines an MPPI planner with a learned residual dynamics model and a low-level RL velocity tracker. The planner augments a nominal unicycle kinematic model with a neural residual term to capture the closed-loop tracking behavior of the RL policy. To train this residual model under limited real-world data, we propose Low-Rank Residual Adaptation (LoRRA), a two-stage approach that pre-trains on large-scale simulation data for broad coverage and then fine-tunes on a small real-world dataset with a low-rank constraint. In simulation, we empirically validate our engineering choices by showing (A) Residual dynamics improve the overall performance of our pipeline by capturing the tracking error of RL velocity tracker at high-speed cornering. (B) Residual dynamics trained with both source-domain and target-domain data gives racing performance significantly better than the residual dynamics trained with only target-domain data. (C) Low-rank constraint at target-domain adaptation gives higher success rates and higher performance than full-tune and from-scratch when domain gap in ground coefficient or joint gain increases.
[中文] 我们提出了RACER ，这是一种轮式四足赛车的分层控制框架，它将MPPI规划器与学习残差动力学模型和低级RL速度跟踪器相结合。规划器使用神经残差项扩充标称独轮车运动学模型，以捕获强化学习策略的闭环跟踪行为。为了在有限的真实世界数据下训练这个残差模型，我们提出了低秩残差自适应（ LoRRA ） ，这是一种两阶段方法，对大规模模拟数据进行预训练以实现广泛的覆盖，然后在具有低秩约束的小型真实世界数据集上进行微调。在仿真中，我们通过展示（ A ）残差动力学通过捕获RL速度跟踪器在高速转弯时的跟踪误差来改善我们管道的整体性能来经验性地验证我们的工程选择。（ B ）使用源域和目标域数据训练的残差动力学使比赛性能明显优于仅使用目标域数据训练的残差动力学。（ C ）当地面系数或联合增益的域间隙增加时，目标域适应的低秩约束比全调和从头开始给予更高的成功率和更高的性能。

Robust Surgical Robotic Instrument Tracking via Sequential Multi-Cue Fusion and Sim-to-Real Self-Training

通过顺序多线索融合和Sim-to-Real自我训练实现强大的手术机器人仪器跟踪
arXiv: 2610.05491 · 2026-10-04 · Hanyang Hu, Zekai Liang, Florian Richter, Michael C. Yip
[EN] Efficient and robust tracking of surgical robotic instruments is important for robot-assisted minimally invasive surgery, yet remains challenging due to the complexity of surgical scenes and the unconventional geometry of surgical instruments. Keypoint-based approaches are efficient, but their performance depends on reliable feature detection. Improving these detectors with real-world supervision is difficult because accurate real-world annotations are costly to obtain at scale. To address this limitation, we introduce a tracker-guided self-training framework that adapts a model pretrained on synthetic images to unlabeled real-world videos. Given measured robot joint states, an uncertainty-aware EKF recursively corrects the instrument pose and the observable joint angles by comparing projected model features with detected keypoints, shaft boundaries, and mask-derived cues. An RTS smoother subsequently refines the resulting trajectory, which is projected into pseudo-labels for fine-tuning the feature detector without laborious pose annotations. Experiments on real-world videos demonstrate consistent improvements from self-training across all evaluated keypoint metrics, and the resulting model outperforms prior approaches in both accuracy and runtime. The code and data will be released upon publication.
[中文] 手术机器人器械的高效稳健跟踪对于机器人辅助微创手术非常重要，但由于手术场景的复杂性和外科器械的非常规几何形状，仍然具有挑战性。基于关键点的方法是有效的，但其性能取决于可靠的特征检测。在真实世界的监督下改进这些探测器很困难，因为大规模获取准确的真实世界注释成本很高。为了解决这一限制，我们引入了一个跟踪器引导的自我训练框架，该框架将合成图像上预训练的模型适应于未标记的现实世界视频。给定测量的机器人关节状态，具有不确定性的EKF通过将投影模型特征与检测到的关键点、轴边界和掩模衍生线索进行比较，递归校正仪器姿态和可观察到的关节角度。RTS平滑器随后细化了生成的轨迹，将其投影到伪标签中，以微调特征检测器，而无需费力的姿势注释。对真实世界视频的实验表明，在所有评估的关键点指标中，自我训练的改进是一致的，所得模型在准确性和运行时间方面都优于先前的方法。代码和数据将在发布后发布。

Difference Feature Map Distillation: Transferring Inter-Sample Relational Knowledge Towards Efficient Transformer-Based Tracking

差异特征图蒸馏：向基于变压器的高效跟踪转移样本间关系知识
arXiv: 2610.05707 · 2026-10-05 · Zhicheng Ding, Xinyu Chu, Qing Tian
[EN] In autonomous driving perception, visual object tracking systems must satisfy stringent latency and power constraints while remaining robust in complex and dynamic environments. Although transformer-based trackers achieve state-of-the-art accuracy, their substantial computational and memory overheads hinder deployment on real-time, resource-constrained platforms. To move toward this goal, we propose Difference Feature Map Knowledge Distillation (DFM-KD), a novel relational distillation framework tailored for transformer-based visual object tracking. Unlike conventional feature distillation methods that minimize point-wise discrepancies (e.g., mean squared error) between teacher and student feature representations, DFM-KD transfers knowledge through inter-sample feature differences, explicitly aligning the relational structure of the feature space. By distilling how the teacher models appearance variation and consistency across samples, rather than enforcing similarity in absolute activations, DFM-KD enables the student to better capture the structural dynamics of visual changes within a batch. As a result, the distilled model exhibits enhanced feature robustness and improved tracking performance. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DFM-KD consistently outperforms conventional feature-level distillation methods in both tracking precision and success rates.
[中文] 在自动驾驶感知中，视觉对象跟踪系统必须满足严格的延迟和功率约束，同时在复杂和动态环境中保持鲁棒性。虽然基于变压器的跟踪器实现了最先进的精度，但其大量的计算和内存开销阻碍了在实时、资源受限的平台上进行部署。为了实现这一目标，我们提出了差分特征图知识蒸馏（ DFM-KD ） ，这是一种为基于变压器的视觉对象跟踪量身定制的新型关系蒸馏框架。与传统的特征提取方法不同， DFM-KD通过样本间特征差异传递知识，显式对齐特征空间的关系结构，从而最小化教师和学生特征表示之间的逐点差异（例如，均方误差）。通过提炼教师如何模拟样本之间的外观变化和一致性，而不是在绝对激活中强制相似性， DFM-KD使学生能够更好地捕捉批内视觉变化的结构动态。因此，蒸馏模型表现出增强的特征鲁棒性和改进的跟踪性能。广泛的实验表明， DFM-KD在跟踪精度和成功率方面始终优于传统的特征级蒸馏方法。
