Distractors are the Real Enemy: Visual Distraction Adversarial Perturbations on SAM2-Based Video Tracking and Segmentation

arXiv: 2611.32011 · 2026-11-26 · Gebremedhin, Adonay Demewez
[EN] Promptable video trackers such as SAM2 achieve accurate and robust performance through strong temporal consistency, making them important foundations for downstream video understanding and tracking applications. As their use continues to expand, understanding their robustness to adversarial perturbations becomes increasingly important. Existing attacks on SAM2 largely focus on local mask degradation and short-term temporal feature disruption, leaving its longer-term dynamics and competing object interferences (Distractors) insufficiently explored. We observe that SAM2 itself exposes useful distractor cues when tracking becomes uncertain. Based on these observations, we propose DistractAttack, a white-box attack that uses background distractors to disrupt target tracking persistently. Its core procedure, Counterfactual Distractor Search (CDS), propagates clean and attacked trajectories in parallel to discover plausible competing regions and identify those that become increasingly effective under perturbation. The discovered evidence is accumulated over time to weaken target support and promote recurrent alternatives or disappearance predictions. Extensive experiments under video-level and one-shot perturbations across Visual Object Tracking and Video Object Segmentation demonstrate strong attack effectiveness, raising DRE to $4.4\times$ and NRE to $7.3\times$ their nearest basel
[中文] SAM2 等可提示视频跟踪器通过强大的时间一致性实现准确且稳健的性能，因此成为下游视频理解和跟踪应用的重要基础。随着其应用范围的不断扩大，了解其对对抗性扰动的鲁棒性变得越来越重要。现有的针对 SAM2 的攻击主要集中在局部掩码退化和短期时间特征破坏方面，而对其长期动态和竞争性对象干扰（干扰项）的探索尚不充分。我们观察到，当跟踪变得不确定时，SAM2 本身会暴露有用的干扰项线索。基于这些观察结果，我们提出了 DistractAttack，这是一种白盒攻击，它使用背景干扰项来持续干扰目标跟踪。其核心程序是反事实干扰项搜索（CDS），该程序并行传播干净轨迹和受攻击轨迹，以发现似是而非的竞争区域，并识别出在扰动下变得越来越有效的区域。随着时间的推移，所发现的证据不断积累，从而削弱对目标的支撑，并促进出现反复出现的替代方案或消失预测。在视觉目标跟踪和视频目标分割中，在视频级和单次扰动下进行了大量实验，结果表明攻击效果显著，DRE 和 NRE 分别提高了至其最近基线的 $4.4\times$ 和 $7.3\times$。

Motion Conditioned World Modeling for Embodied Visual Tracking

用于具身视觉跟踪的运动条件化世界建模
arXiv: 2611.32900 · 2026-11-24 · Jiahao Zhang, Xuan Yao, Junyu Gao, Changsheng Xu
[EN] World models support embodied decision-making by predicting future environment states. In embodied visual tracking, uncertain target motion and robot-induced viewpoint changes jointly affect visual observations, making future-state prediction challenging. We propose Motion-Conditioned World Modeling, which incorporates predicted target-motion distributions and robot ego-motion into latent state transitions. A Geometry--Progress module jointly models geometric paths and single-step travel distances through shared motion modes and path-conditioned progress distributions. This formulation captures uncertainty in route selection and motion progress while preserving their dependence. Action-induced coordinate transformations express predicted paths in the next robot frame, providing geometrically grounded conditions for future visual-state prediction. Future visual feature reconstruction supervises the motion predictor through the conditioned transition branch, linking explicit motion representations to subsequent observations. The model is jointly optimized with motion likelihood, feature reconstruction, and expert-action supervision, enabling the tracking policy to combine visual context with predictive motion cues.Experiments on three embodied tracking benchmarks demonstrate improved tracking performance and cross-environment generalization with a lightweight $0.5$B backbone.

MRCT: An Object-Centric Two-Layer Control Architecture for Heavy-Payload Multi-Robot Transportation

MRCT：一种面向物体的双层控制架构，用于重载多机器人运输
arXiv: 2611.32502 · 2026-11-24 · Ning Wang, Yoshihiko Nakamura, Tianwei Zhang
[EN] Cooperative transportation of heavy payloads by heterogeneous mobile manipulators requires coordinated payload motion, force distribution, and compliant physical interaction. This paper presents MRCT, an object-centric two-layer control architecture for multi-robot transportation. At the object level, a model predictive controller (MPC) generates a coordinated contact-force reference for the payload. At the robot level, whole- body impedance control (WBIC) modules on individual robots realise the reference through a confidence-based blending of velocity-tracking and impedance torques. The blending mechanism emphasises tracking when the joint-velocity error is small and increases compliance when tracking degrades because of abrupt reference changes or physical disturbances. Simulation and hardware experiments with a heterogeneous team comprising a single-arm and a dual-arm mobile manipulator transporting a near-rated table payload demonstrate accurate object tracking, regulated contact forces, and stable payload orientation. Parameter sensitivity studies, obstacle-avoidance transportation, and step-load disturbance experiments further demonstrate the effectiveness of MRCT for reliable cooperative transportation under the evaluated conditions
[中文] 异构移动操纵器协同运输重型负载需要协调的负载运动、力分配和柔顺的物理交互。本文介绍了 MRCT，这是一种用于多机器人搬运的以物体为中心的两层控制架构。在物体层级，模型预测控制器（MPC）为负载生成协调的接触力参考。在机器人层面，各台机器人上的全身阻抗控制（WBIC）模块通过基于置信度的速度跟踪和阻抗扭矩的融合来实现参考。当关节速度误差较小时，融合机制强调跟踪；当由于基准值的突然变化或物理扰动而导致跟踪性能下降时，则增加柔顺性。由单臂和双臂移动机械手组成的异构团队对接近额定值的台面负载进行了模拟和硬件实验，展示了准确的物体跟踪、受控的接触力和稳定的负载方向。参数敏感性研究、避障运输和阶跃负载扰动实验进一步证明了MRCT在所评估条件下实现可靠协同运输的有效性。

WorldWeave: Rethinking Embodied Navigation through Cross-Task World Modeling

WorldWeave：通过跨任务世界建模重新思考具身导航
arXiv: 2611.28553 · 2026-11-21 · Xuan Yao, Jiahao Zhang, Rongjunchen Zhang, Kaixuan Huang, Junyu Gao, Changsheng Xu
[EN] Mobile embodied agents continuously alter their observations through their own motion, while external dynamics further shape future interactions. Different mobile embodied tasks therefore capture complementary patterns of state evolution, yet jointly learning world dynamics across such interactions remains less explored. We investigate this problem through Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) and Embodied Visual Tracking (EVT), which respectively involve navigation toward a stationary goal and interaction with a moving target. We propose WorldWeave, a cross-task world model that couples shared predictive dynamics with task-specific adaptation. Instead of conditioning state transitions on heterogeneous physical actions, WorldWeave learns latent actions from observed state changes via inverse-forward dynamics, establishing a shared transition representation across tasks. The resulting future-state predictions are adaptively integrated into task-specific representations for downstream decision making. Experiments across multiple VLN and EVT benchmarks demonstrate consistent improvements over specialized baselines.
[中文] 移动具身智能体通过自身的运动不断改变其观察，而外部动态则进一步塑造未来的交互。因此，不同的移动具身任务捕捉到了状态演变的互补模式，但跨越此类交互共同学习世界动态的研究仍较少。我们通过视觉-语言导航（VLN）和具身视觉跟踪（EVT）来研究这个问题，前者涉及朝向静止目标导航，后者涉及与移动目标互动。我们提出了 WorldWeave，这是一种跨任务世界模型，将共享的预测动态与任务特定的适应相结合。WorldWeave 不会将状态转换与异构物理动作挂钩，而是通过逆向-正向动力学从观察到的状态变化中学习潜在动作，从而建立跨任务的共享转换表示。由此产生的未来状态预测会被自适应地整合到特定于任务的表示中，用于下游决策。跨多个VLN和EVT基准的实验表明，与专用基线相比，性能得到了持续的提升。

Tracking Objects Through Assembly and Decomposition

通过组装和分解跟踪对象
arXiv: 2611.26269 · 2026-11-19 · Cheryl Lam, Yihong Sun, Bharath Hariharan
[EN] Tracking objects through transformations is essential for understanding real-world dynamics. Recent work has extended traditional object tracking to detect state changes. However, existing methods require an initial object prompt and detect only its decompositions, missing assembly events, which begin with multiple objects rather than one. We therefore introduce a prompt-free formulation of this task, where the goal is to recover all object tracks and detect both decomposition and assembly events given only a video. Our key insight is that assembly is decomposition in reverse. Building on this insight, we present Atlas, a zero-shot system that constructs and jointly reasons over forward and reverse spatiotemporal partitions to recover object tracks and their transformations. We further introduce OmniST, a benchmark with dense annotations of objects and both transformation types. On OmniST, Atlas recovers more than twice as many state changes as prior methods while remaining competitive on existing benchmarks that only evaluate decomposition.

Choose What Returns: Object-Aware KV Memory for Selective Recall in Interactive Video Generation

选择什么内容返回：用于交互式视频生成中进行选择性检索的对象感知型 KV 内存
arXiv: 2611.28137 · 2026-11-19 · Hyunwook Kang, Junoh Park, Hyunggyu Lim, Taesup Kim
[EN] Interactive long-video generation lets users build a story through successive prompts and reuse earlier characters and settings under new instructions. Existing memory methods retain past content, yet they often fail at precise selective recall, returning the wrong appearance of a person, mixing different people, or keeping an unrequested background. Based on our analysis of memory form and retrieval, we introduce an object-aware key--value (KV) memory framework for a frozen autoregressive diffusion generator. It keeps the original KV of tracked object regions, selects background KV outside them by attention-scores, and links each memory with a readable record of its source scene and observed appearance. An LLM selects the requested records, and a memory integrator supplies their KV for generation under the current prompt.
