CoHelix: Event-Based Multi-Propeller Rotation-Rate Tracking with Shared Projective Geometry

CoHelix：具有共享投影几何的基于事件的多螺旋桨旋转速率跟踪
arXiv: 2610.31384 · 2026-10-22 · Radim Spetlik, Tobiáš Vinklárek, Jan Klivan, Tomáš Kestřánek, Matouš Vrba, Jiří Matas
[EN] Estimating propeller rates from a moving event camera requires separating blade rotation from the changing image geometry. Background activity mixes with blade events, while repeated blade appearances admit half- and double-rate ambiguities. We propose CoHelix, a recursive filter that estimates one shared homography and separate rotation states for four rigidly mounted, coplanar propellers. Each admitted event updates its assigned rotation state, and its accepted geometry measurements correct the shared homography used to interpret later events from every propeller. We report lock, the fraction of evaluated time for which an estimate remains within 2% of synchronized ground truth, because mean error alone does not show how long a tracker follows the true rate. With fixed-rate initialization, leave-one-recording-out evaluation on moving-camera data gives CoHelix 62.9% lock, compared with 39.8% for HelixTrack and 22.2% for EventPro. With other settings fixed, allowing four geometry contributors instead of one raises lock from 3.4% to 62.9%. On a private target-follower dataset with a different target quadcopter, CoHelix retains the measurement settings selected on moving-camera data and uses a fixed initial rate. It reaches 95.9% lock, compared with 56.2% for HelixTrack and 61.1% for EventPro.
[中文] 从移动事件摄像机估计螺旋桨速率需要将叶片旋转与不断变化的图像几何形状分开。背景活动与刀片事件混合在一起，而重复的刀片出现则允许半速率和双速率的模糊性。我们提出了 CoHelix，一种递归滤波器，可以估计四个刚性安装的共面螺旋桨的一个共享单应性和单独的旋转状态。每个承认的事件都会更新其指定的旋转状态，并且其接受的几何测量会纠正用于解释来自每个螺旋桨的后续事件的共享单应性。我们报告锁定，即估计值与同步地面实况的 2% 以内的估计时间的比例，因为平均误差本身并不能显示跟踪器遵循真实速率的时间。通过固定速率初始化，对移动摄像机数据的留一记录评估为 CoHelix 提供了 62.9% 的锁定，而 HelixTrack 为 39.8%，EventPro 为 22.2%。在其他设置固定的情况下，允许四个几何贡献者而不是一个将锁定从 3.4% 提高到 62.9%。在具有不同目标四轴飞行器的私有目标跟随数据集上，CoHelix 保留在移动摄像机数据上选择的测量设置并使用固定的初始速率。它达到了 95.9% 的锁定，而 HelixTrack 为 56.2%，EventPro 为 61.1%。

Inside-JEPA: From Prediction to Relational Grounding in Latent World Models

Inside-JEPA：从预测到潜在世界模型中的关系基础
arXiv: 2610.31194 · 2026-10-22 · Hossein Resani, Javen Qinfeng Shi
[EN] Joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPAs) learn world models in latent space, enabling efficient planning but leaving their states opaque. We introduce Inside-JEPA, which places relational channels for ordered entity pairs inside the JEPA latent state and shows that an inverse-dynamics loss can ground these channels without relation labels when its head reads them, either alone or as part of the full state. On Something-Something V2, latent prediction alone performs no better than a random relation head, while inverse-dynamics supervision that recovers actions from channel changes improves relational AUROC by .065-.103 across ten settings with annotated boxes, by .081 on RH20T robot episodes with held-out tasks, and by .050-.061 across five relations using off-the-shelf detection and tracking. An inverse head on entity latents decodes actions more accurately but barely improves relational grounding; shuffled labels, two label-free objectives, and action labels on only 5% of the pairs also fail to ground the channels. Given an action, predicted relational changes approach the quality of observed future relations (AUROC .599 vs .621). For one-step relational goals, a supervised readout of the 16 channels ranks the correct action among the top five of 65 in 43% of cases, compared with 24% for prediction-only latents and 17% for a frequency prior.
[中文] 联合嵌入预测架构（JEPA）在潜在空间中学习世界模型，从而实现高效规划，但使其状态不透明。我们介绍了 Inside-JEPA，它将有序实体对的关系通道放置在 JEPA 潜在状态内，并表明，当其头部单独或作为完整状态的一部分读取这些通道时，逆动态损失可以在没有关系标签的情况下接地这些通道。在 Something-Something V2 上，单独的潜在预测的表现并不比随机关系头更好，而从通道变化中恢复动作的逆动态监督在带注释框的 10 个设置中将关系 AUROC 提高了 0.065-0.103，在带有保留任务的 RH20T 机器人情节中提高了 0.081，在使用现成检测和跟踪的五个关系中提高了 0.050-0.061。实体潜伏的逆向头可以更准确地解码动作，但几乎无法改善关系基础；打乱标签、两个无标签目标以及仅 5% 对的动作标签也无法使通道接地。给定一个动作，预测的关系变化接近观察到的未来关系的质量（AUROC .599 vs .621）。对于一步关系目标，16 个通道的监督读出在 43% 的情况下将正确操作排在 65 个通道中的前 5 名，而仅预测潜在变量的正确率为 24%，频率先验的正确操作为 17%。

Learning-Enhanced Observer for Sensorless Task-Null-Space Compliant Control

用于无传感器任务零空间兼容控制的学习增强观察器
arXiv: 2610.28769 · 2026-10-20 · Nuo Chen, Ya-Jun Pan, Onur Toker
[EN] This paper presents a novel framework integrating a robust wrench observer with a hybrid pose and force controller for physical Human-Robot Interaction (pHRI). In pHRI tasks, robots are expected to perform task-space motions robustly while remaining compliance with human disturbance, which proposes distinct control objectives. Most existing approaches focus mainly on either robustness or compliance and rely on force sensors for real-time tracking. To address these limitations, we propose a novel framework with the following key contributions. Firstly, a neural network (NN) is trained to improve the observer performance by compensating for system friction and unmodeled dynamics. Secondly, a task-null-space decomposition is applied to separate the joint velocity and reduce the effect of the human disturbance on the observer. Finally, a hybrid pose and force controller is introduced to achieve the end-effector pose and force tracking. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework provides robust and accurate wrench estimation while maintaining reliable pose and force tracking performance. The proposed approach offers an effective solution and framework for pHRI without the need of force/torque sensor measurements which can be applied to many robotic manipulator platforms which do not have force/torque sensors.
[中文] 本文提出了一种新颖的框架，将稳健的扳手观察器与混合姿势和力控制器相结合，用于物理人机交互（pHRI）。在 pHRI 任务中，机器人预计能够稳健地执行任务空间运动，同时保持遵守人类干扰，这提出了不同的控制目标。大多数现有方法主要关注稳健性或合规性，并依靠力传感器进行实时跟踪。为了解决这些限制，我们提出了一个具有以下主要贡献的新颖框架。首先，训练神经网络（NN），通过补偿系统摩擦和未建模的动态来提高观察者的性能。其次，应用任务零空间分解来分离关节速度并减少人类干扰对观察者的影响。最后，引入混合姿态和力控制器来实现末端执行器的姿态和力跟踪。实验结果表明，所提出的框架提供了稳健且准确的扳手估计，同时保持了可靠的姿态和力跟踪性能。所提出的方法为 pHRI 提供了一种有效的解决方案和框架，无需力/扭矩传感器测量，可应用于许多没有力/扭矩传感器的机器人操纵器平台。

AeroDASH: Asynchronous Vision-Language-Action Control for Continuous UAV Flight

AeroDASH：用于连续无人机飞行的异步视觉-语言-动作控制
arXiv: 2610.25407 · 2026-10-18 · Zhe Liu, Yuxuan Zheng, Jinzhe Qin, Ziqi Gao, Boyu Zhou
[EN] Onboard inference latency creates a mismatch between the trajectories predicted by vision-language-action (VLA) policies and the ongoing motion of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). We present AeroDASH, an asynchronous VLA framework for continuous language-conditioned flight. A B-spline policy is initialized through supervised fine-tuning on expert demonstrations, providing continuous trajectory references in position and yaw. During execution, trajectory fusion removes the expired prediction prefix and connects the remaining segment to the active reference with derivative continuity while limiting trajectory deformation. To account for deviations induced by asynchronous execution, execution-aware group relative policy optimization (GRPO) further adapts the policy using trajectory-level rewards. On the UAV-Flow test set, AeroDASH achieves a success rate of 76.56%, exceeding OpenVLA-UAV and AeroDASH w/o GRPO by 38.1 and 20.1 percentage points, respectively. Execution experiments show that AeroDASH sustains motion during inference and reduces acceleration, jerk, and speed fluctuations relative to asynchronous tracking of waypoint sequences. We further demonstrate language-conditioned flight with onboard inference and asynchronous trajectory execution. The code will be released upon acceptance.
[中文] 机载推理延迟会导致视觉-语言-动作 (VLA) 策略预测的轨迹与无人机 (UAV) 的持续运动之间不匹配。我们推出了 AeroDASH，这是一个用于连续语言条件飞行的异步 VLA 框架。 B 样条策略通过专家演示的监督微调来初始化，提供位置和偏航的连续轨迹参考。在执行过程中，轨迹融合会删除过期的预测前缀，并将剩余段连接到具有导数连续性的活动参考，同时限制轨迹变形。为了解决异步执行引起的偏差，执行感知组相对策略优化（GRPO）使用轨迹级奖励进一步调整策略。在UAV-Flow测试集上，AeroDASH的成功率达到76.56%，分别超过OpenVLA-UAV和AeroDASH w/o GRPO 38.1和20.1个百分点。执行实验表明，与异步跟踪航路点序列相比，AeroDASH 在推理期间维持运动并减少加速度、急动度和速度波动。我们进一步演示了具有机载推理和异步轨迹执行的语言条件飞行。该代码将在接受后发布。

Foundation4D: Towards a unified perception and reconstruction model for autonomous vehicles

Foundation4D：迈向自动驾驶汽车的统一感知和重建模型
arXiv: 2610.30340 · 2026-10-21 · NVIDIA, :, Sérgio Agostinho, Maximilian Apodaca, Emmanuel Attia, Guillem Braso
[EN] We introduce Foundation4D, a unified feed-forward model for geometry estimation, 3D detection, semantic occupancy, and Gaussian reconstruction from multi-camera driving video. A shared backbone uses factorized spatiotemporal attention to efficiently combine observations across cameras and time. Our video inference pipeline uses pose graph optimization to refine camera trajectories and assembles object tracks across input windows. We also develop a data annotation pipeline and release PAI-Foundation4D, a benchmark with dense depth and semantic occupancy labels for 1,253 driving clips. With benchmark-specific fine-tuning, Foundation4D achieves the lowest depth AbsRel on Waymo and nuScenes, the highest detection mAP on nuScenes, and the highest occupancy mIoU on OpenOccupancy among the compared methods. Our task-combination study shows benefits for both detection and geometry: co-training detection with geometry and occupancy improves top-class mAP by 1.2 points over detection-only training, while co-training geometry with detection reduces camera-to-rig translation error by $13.4\%$ relative to geometry-only training. We further use the predicted geometry and trajectories as priors for lidarless NuRec, supporting reconstruction of driving scenes for simulation.
