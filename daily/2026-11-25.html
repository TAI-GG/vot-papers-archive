Test-Time Selective Adaptation for Cross-Modal UAV Object Tracking with Token Shift

基于令牌偏移的跨模态无人机目标跟踪的测试时有选择性自适应
2611.15059 · 2026-11-25 · Xiao, Yun
[EN] Cross-modal unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) object tracking faces modality-induced appearance discrepancies and abrupt target-position shifts that can arise from modality switching, UAV motion, and inter-sensor offsets. Although existing methods model modality and spatial variations, they primarily rely on offline representation learning and offer limited localization-oriented adaptation during testing. To address these challenges, we propose Test-Time Selective Adaptation with Token Shift (TSAT), which derives spatial and temporal adaptation signals from unlabeled test sequences for online optimization. Specifically, we design a controllable token shift auxiliary task, which imposes explicit spatial correspondence between predefined token translations and resultant localization offsets, generating stable annotation-free spatial adaptation signals. Furthermore, we introduce a temporal backward prediction auxiliary task to exploit frame-wise temporal dependencies, effectively mitigating cumulative localization errors in continuous tracking. To suppress interference from unreliable tracking samples, we develop a reliability-aware update gating strategy to screen valid adaptation signals and stabilize online parameter updates. Experiments on CM-UOT demonstrate that TSAT achieves state-of-the-art performance against existing test-time adaptation methods, exhibiting superior robustnes
[中文] 跨模态无人机（UAV）目标跟踪面临着模态引起的外观差异以及目标位置的突然偏移，这些情况可能由模态切换、无人机运动和传感器间偏移所致。虽然现有方法对模态和空间变化进行了建模，但它们主要依赖于离线表征学习，并且在测试过程中提供的定位导向性自适应能力有限。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了具有标记位移的测试时选择性自适应（TSAT），它从未标记的测试序列中提取空间和时间自适应信号，用于在线优化。具体而言，我们设计了一个可控的标记位移辅助任务，该任务在预定义的标记位移与由此产生的定位偏移之间建立了明确的空间对应关系，从而生成稳定的无标注空间自适应信号。此外，我们引入了时间后向预测辅助任务，以利用逐帧的时间依赖性，从而有效减轻连续跟踪中的累积定位误差。为了抑制来自不可靠跟踪样本的干扰，我们开发了一种可靠性感知更新门控策略，以筛选有效的自适应信号并稳定在线参数更新。在 CM-UOT 上的实验表明，与现有的测试时自适应方法相比，TSAT 实现了最先进的性能，表现出卓越的鲁棒性。

EM-SDTrack: Efficient Multi-Scale Spike-driven Tracking for Event Cameras

EM-SDTrack：用于事件相机的多尺度高效脉冲驱动跟踪
2611.22700 · 2026-11-25 · Shan, Yimeng
[EN] Event-driven spiking neural networks (SNNs) match the sparse spatiotemporal output of event cameras, making spike-driven trackers highly attractive for neuromorphic edge deployment. However, events reveal a target only through their spatial distribution, whose extent varies more than tenfold within one sequence. Event tracking therefore demands multi-scale representation, which existing spike-driven trackers lack. Transferring multi-scale schemes from artificial neural networks either breaks spike-driven inference with floating-point multiplication or repeats backbone computation across input scales. To address this, we propose Spike-driven Multi-Scale Interaction (SDMI), which performs cross-scale interaction in a single spiking forward pass. Its core, Dyadic Spike Sampling (DSS), restricts sampling coordinates to dyadic fractions and thus requires only shifts and additions. The resulting M-SDTrack is fully spike-driven at inference. For tighter edge budgets, we further propose Polarity-Privileged Distillation (PPD), which distils M-SDTrack into EM-SDTrack. PPD treats the event counts cancelled by the voxel representation as privileged information. Its core constraint, Polarity-Guided Curvature Distillation (PGCD), aligns the curvature of the template-search relation under polarity-guided perturbations. On FE108, FELT and VisEvent, M-SDTrack achieves state-of-the-art accuracy
