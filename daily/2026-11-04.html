Latent Spectral Prompting for Multimodal Hyperspectral Object Tracking

2611.02512 · 2026-11-02 · Wangshu Cai, Jun Zhou, Lei Wang, Mohammad Aminul Islam, Xu Han, Kuldip Paliwal
[EN] Hyperspectral videos provide rich spectral signatures for robust tracking under challenging conditions such as camouflage and low illumination. However, limited hyperspectral annotations and the large RGB-hyperspectral domain gap hinder the direct use of modern temporally pretrained RGB trackers. We propose SProTrack, a multimodal framework that transfers temporal knowledge from pretrained RGB trackers to the hyperspectral domain through latent spectral prompting. The key idea is to decouple temporal modeling from spectral adaptation, allowing temporal dynamics to be inherited from RGB foundation models while hyperspectral information is learned from unlabeled data in a self-supervised manner. Unlike band-selection methods that discard most spectral information or prompt-based approaches that rely on local-search crops, SProTrack learns a global, scene-level spectral representation from the full hyperspectral frame using an autoencoder. This latent representation is used to guide spectral prompt extraction while a pretrained RGB tracker preserves strong temporal modeling on RGB-compatible inputs. By encoding spectral-spatial structure into compact latent prompts, SProTrack bridges the RGB-hyperspectral modality gap without extensive retraining or dense supervision.
