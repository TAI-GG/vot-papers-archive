The Impact of Detector Architecture, Model Scale, and Scene Density on Multi-Pedestrian Tracking

探测器架构、模型规模和场景密度对多人跟踪的影响
2610.19369 · 2026-10-14 · Pedro Manoel Herminio Alves, Dalton Cézane Gomes Valadares, Cláudio de Souza Baptista
[EN] Pedestrian tracking in video is important for applications such as surveillance and scene analysis, but it becomes more challenging in crowded scenarios with frequent occlusions and rapid motion. This paper analyzes how detector architecture, model scale, scene density, and inference regime affect the tracking of multiple pedestrians. We compare the YOLO11, RT-DETR, and YOLO26 detector families with the ByteTrack and BoT-SORT trackers on sequences from the MOT17, MOT20, and SOMPT22 benchmarks. For YOLO26, we also compare traditional overlap filtering via Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) with end-to-end inference. The RT-DETR family achieved the highest mean scores on the HOTA tracking quality metric across all three benchmarks with both trackers, with a more pronounced advantage on MOT20, albeit at a higher computational cost. On MOT17 and SOMPT22, YOLO26 variants, especially YOLO26-L with NMS, offered favorable combinations of quality and speed compared with RT-DETR. In the YOLO26 inference comparison, the end-to-end mode did not yield consistent tracking gains over the NMS-based regime. Taken together, these results provide a practical guide for selecting detectors and trackers for this task based on application priorities.
[中文] 视频中的行人跟踪对于监控和场景分析等应用非常重要，但在频繁遮挡和快速运动的拥挤场景中变得更具挑战性。本文分析了检测器架构、模型规模、场景密度和推理机制如何影响多个行人的跟踪。我们将 YOLO11、RT-DETR 和 YOLO26 检测器系列与 ByteTrack 和 BoT-SORT 跟踪器在 MOT17、MOT20 和 SOMPT22 基准测试的序列上进行比较。对于 YOLO26，我们还将通过非极大值抑制 (NMS) 的传统重叠过滤与端到端推理进行比较。RT-DETR 系列在两个跟踪器的所有三个基准测试中均取得了 HOTA 跟踪质量指标的最高平均分数，在 MOT20 上具有更明显的优势，尽管计算成本较高。在 MOT17 和 SOMPT22 上，YOLO26 变体，尤其是带有 NMS 的 YOLO26-L，与 RT-DETR 相比提供了良好的质量和速度组合。在 YOLO26 推理比较中，与基于 NMS 的机制相比，端到端模式没有产生一致的跟踪增益。总而言之，这些结果为根据应用优先级选择用于此任务的探测器和跟踪器提供了实用指南。

VLX-Go: Robust Embodied Tracking through Multi-Stage Learning and On-Policy Refinement

VLX-Go：通过多阶段学习和策略细化实现稳健的体现跟踪
2610.19361 · 2026-10-14 · Kyusong Lee, Heting Ying, Tiancheng Zhao
[EN] Embodied visual tracking requires a robot to identify a designated target from visual observations and control its own motion to follow that target through an environment. Following an isolated person is a comparatively simple demonstration of embodied visual tracking, but following the intended person among distractors remains challenging. Existing methods exhibit a substantial drop in success from single-target to distractor tracking, motivating a focus on the shared spaces that person-following robots must navigate. We present \method{}, a vision--language waypoint policy built around the hypothesis that visual and language representations should be aligned before tracking-specific motion is learned. The policy combines complementary frozen visual encoders, multiscale temporal context, a transformer backbone, and a lightweight head for robot-centric waypoint prediction. Its training progresses through vision--language alignment, multimodal reasoning mid-training, and tracking specialization on 19,532 oracle episodes. Compared with 41.54\% distractor-tracking success for our earlier OmTrackVLA model, the complete multi-stage recipe achieves 55.80\%. We then explore on-policy refinement using filtered oracle labels for states visited by the policy. This preliminary study adds 2.42 percentage points of distractor-tracking success, reaching 58.22\%, while reducing collision rate
