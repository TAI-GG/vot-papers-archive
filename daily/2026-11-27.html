Towards 360$^\circ$ Embodied UAV Tracking Without Turning

迈向360$^\circ$ 体现无人机不转弯跟踪
2611.33934 · 2026-11-27 · Li, Bingyu
[EN] Conventional embodied UAV tracking couples target observability with heading control because targets may leave the field of view of forward-facing cameras. We investigate a complementary paradigm that uses full-spherical perception to follow designated targets without active heading adjustment. To support this setting, we introduce 360UAV-ETrack, a simulation benchmark comprising 2,652 sequences, over 1.73 million synchronized observations, eight tracking tasks, and 150 held-out closed-loop scenarios. We propose 360UAV-TVLA, a panoramic vision--language--action (VLA) framework integrating persistent visual referring (PVR), compressed temporal memory (CTM), and geometry-aligned trajectory supervision (GTS) for instance identification, motion reasoning, and three-dimensional control. Experiments across two VLA backbones show consistent gains over standard fine-tuning, with normalized average tracking frames increasing from 22.56\% to 35.28\% for $π_0$ and from 25.68\% to 33.64\% for $π_{0.5}$. Our benchmark further reveals a substantial gap between offline imitation accuracy and closed-loop tracking reliability, establishing a challenging testbed for future research https://libingyu01.github.io/360UAV-ETrack_360UAV-TVLA/

TaCo: Rethinking Correlation for Visual Tracking on Edge Devices

2611.34084 · 2026-11-25 · Qingmao Wei, Fagui Liu, Quan Tang
[EN] Lightweight trackers have replaced cross-correlation with attention, and on low-power edge hardware they pay for it, since memory-bound softmax, normalization and reshaping operators of attention cost far more time than their arithmetic suggests. Benchmarking efficient trackers on an edge Neural Processing Unit (NPU) and an Arm CPU under one numerically validated protocol, we find that correlation trackers are the fast ones, but they lag in accuracy unless they adopt a ViT backbone, which brings attention back. We revisit what correlation lacks. Template--search interaction serves two roles: conditioning the search features on the target and matching them against it spatially. Attention does both, whereas correlation only matches, and does so on features extracted without knowledge of the target. We present TaCo (Target-aligned Correlation), which supplies the missing conditioning without attention by letting the template enter the per-frame network only as operands computed once per video. A descriptor of the target scales and shifts the search features channel by channel inside the backbone, and kernels aligned with the target box, each with a weight predicted from the template, correlate with them in one matrix product. Recall4D: Forgetting, Not Misalignment, Limits Long-Horizon Feed-Forward 4D Reconstruction

Recall4D：遗忘而非错位限制了长视野前馈 4D 重建
2611.33655 · 2026-11-25 · Tianle Lyu
[EN] Feed-forward 4D trackers recover camera motion, depth, and point trajectories from short video clips, but chaining windows over long videos degrades their accuracy. We find that replacing inter-window alignment with ground-truth transforms leaves tracking error essentially unchanged. Static-point error instead grows with time since last observation: repeatedly predicting a static point from windows that no longer observe it corrupts previously recovered geometry. We call this failure forgetting. Merely retaining the last observed positions of unseen points reduces error by 26% on PointOdyssey without modifying the tracker. We introduce Recall4D, a training-free framework that augments a frozen tracker with a persistent world state and sparse motion anchors. Static points retain their world-space positions outside the camera view and are refined by averaging observations decoded at sparse keyframes while in view. For dynamic objects, the tracker decodes sparse representative anchors, while geometric skinning propagates their displacements to dense queries. Across two long-horizon benchmarks, Recall4D improves the accuracy of its backbone while running 7.6-9.0$\times$ faster, and its accuracy gains carry over to five further backbones and three other long-sequence tasks, demonstrating its generality.
[中文] 前馈 4D 跟踪器可以从短视频剪辑中恢复相机运动、深度和点轨迹，但长视频上的链接窗口会降低其准确性。我们发现，用真实变换替换窗口间对齐使得跟踪误差基本上保持不变。相反，自上次观察以来，静态点误差随着时间的推移而增长：从不再观察静态点的窗口中重复预测静态点会破坏之前恢复的几何图形。我们称之为失败遗忘。仅保留未见点的最后观察位置即可在 PointOdyssey 上减少 26% 的误差，而无需修改跟踪器。我们引入了 Recall4D，这是一个免训练的框架，它通过持久的世界状态和稀疏的运动锚来增强冻结的跟踪器。静态点保留其在摄像机视图之外的世界空间位置，并通过对视图中稀疏关键帧解码的观测值进行平均来进行细化。对于动态对象，跟踪器对稀疏代表性锚点进行解码，而几何蒙皮将其位移传播到密集查询。在两个长范围基准测试中，Recall4D 提高了其骨干网的准确性，同时运行速度提高了 7.6-9.0$\times$，并且其准确性的提高还适用于另外五个骨干网和其他三个长序列任务，这证明了其通用性。
